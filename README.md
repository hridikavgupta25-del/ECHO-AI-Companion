# ECHO — AI Companion

> **It doesn't just remember the story. It remembers YOU.**

ECHO is a sci-fi exploration mystery game featuring a memory-driven AI companion.  
The player explores an abandoned research facility while ECHO observes their decisions, remembers their actions, and adapts its dialogue and behavior accordingly.

---

## 🎮 Game Overview

You wake up inside an abandoned research facility with no clear idea of what happened.

An AI system named **ECHO** is still active.

As you explore the facility, you will:

- 🔍 Investigate objects and hidden clues
- 🧩 Solve a power-access puzzle
- 💬 Communicate with ECHO
- 🧠 Make choices that affect ECHO's trust in you
- 👁️ Discover information about the facility
- 🚪 Unlock new areas
- 🎯 Make a final decision about whether to trust ECHO

The way you interact with ECHO influences how it responds to you.

---

## 🧠 The AI Companion

ECHO uses a **memory-driven adaptive dialogue system**.

Instead of treating every interaction as independent, the game maintains information about the player's actions.

### ECHO remembers:

- **Trust** — How much ECHO trusts the player
- **Curiosity** — How actively the player investigates
- **Discovered Clues** — Important information found during exploration
- **Player Choices** — Previous decisions made by the player
- **Current Objective** — The player's current progress

These values influence ECHO's responses during important interactions.

### Example

A player who:

> Investigates clues + questions ECHO + builds trust

can receive different dialogue from a player who:

> Ignores clues + distrusts ECHO + avoids questioning it.

This creates a more personalized gameplay experience.
